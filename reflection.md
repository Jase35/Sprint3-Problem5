The application helped deepen my understanding of the topic by showing how hackers can exploit unforeseen observable outputs to reduce the amount of login attempts - in this case, reducing O(k^n) to O(k * n). This makes a tremendous difference, as fewer attempts are far more difficult to flag as malicious. The step-by-step format of the page also really helps to break things down and promote a well-rounded understanding of the vulnerability.
For development, high-level decisions were determined by human specification, while a lot of the implementation was left to GitHub Copilot to expedite development.
